Welcome to the Garry'sMod Fan Hub

Garry's Mod (GMod) is a physics sandbox built on Valve's Source engine. There's no set goal — you spawn props, ragdolls, NPCs, vehicles, and tools, then glue it all together with wire, welds, and raw creativity. Thanks to the Steam Workshop, it's been extended for nearly two decades with mods for building, roleplay, combat, and total nonsense. This page rounds up the best of it.

Building Tips

Practical habits that separate a stable contraption from a pile of props waiting to explode.

01

Learn the tool gun context menu

Hold Q for the spawn menu and use the Tool tab to browse every tool's options before spawning anything — most tools have settings (mode, constraint type, force limits) that are easy to miss if you never open the menu.

02

Weld vs. nocollide — know the difference

Weld locks two props together rigidly, like one solid object. Nocollide just stops two props from colliding but lets them move independently. Use weld for a chassis, nocollide for overlapping parts like suspension arms or hinged panels that need to pass through each other.

03

Freeze props while you stack them

Right-click with the Physics Gun to freeze a prop in place. Build your stack frozen, then unfreeze (or weld) once everything is positioned — it stops half-placed props from sliding or falling before you're done.

04

Use the Axis tool for anything that rotates

Doors, turrets, wheels, and cranes need the Axis tool, not a weld. Set friction and torque limits deliberately — too little friction and things spin freely under gravity, too much and motors can't move the joint at all.

05

Start Wiremod simple

Before writing Expression 2 code, get comfortable with basic gates: a button wired to a gate, a gate wired to a thruster or motor. Understanding signal flow with simple parts makes E2 scripting click a lot faster later.

06

Keep contraptions light and low

Source's physics engine gets unstable with heavy, top-heavy, or high-prop-count builds. Lower your center of mass, avoid stacking dozens of heavy props unsupported, and test with a nudge (or a small explosion) before adding more weight.

07

Pick the right joint for the job

Rope and elastic for tension (suspension, cranes), ballsocket for flexible pivots (limbs, swinging arms), weld for anything that must stay perfectly rigid. Mixing these deliberately is how stable-but-flexible builds are made.

08

Save early, save often

Use Advanced Duplicator 2 (or the built-in Duplicator) to save contraptions to disk regularly. Server crashes and map changes will erase unsaved work, and there's nothing worse than losing a two-hour build to a map restart.

09

Use grid snap / Precision tools for symmetry

Eyeballing alignment gets old fast. Precision-style tools project a measurement grid onto props so you can align, space, and rotate things to exact values — much faster than nudging with the physics gun.

10

Learn a few keyboard shortcuts

Q spawn menu, C context menu (for the Physics Gun's constraint options), R is the default rope tool bind, and scroll wheel adjusts most tool ranges/values — learning these saves constant menu-diving mid-build.

Fun Things to Do When Bored

No objective, no problem. Solo or with friends, here's what to do when you just opened GMod with no plan.

🎯

Play Prop Hunt

Grab a Prop Hunt server, disguise yourself as a traffic cone, and try not to move when a hunter walks by. Somehow still tense after a decade.

🚗

Build a working vehicle

Combine simfphys with Wiremod to build a car with working headlights, a horn, and a speedometer. Then find the biggest ramp on the map.

🤸

Ragdoll physics experiments

Launch ragdolls off cliffs with the physics gun, stack them into pyramids, or rig up a catapult. The ragdoll physics never stop being funny.

🗺️

Explore community maps

Beyond gm_construct, the Workshop has huge cities, flooded facilities, and sprawling sandboxes built by mappers. Noclip around and see what people have made.

⚔️

Stage an NPC battle

Spawn Combine soldiers against rebels (or zombies against everyone) and watch the AI fight it out — bonus points for building a fortified base for one side first.

🎬

Try machinima

Use playermodels, PAC3, and the built-in camera/demo tools to block out a scene and record a short film. GMod's roots as a machinima tool are still fully intact.

🕹️

Play TTT or DarkRP with friends

Nothing beats the social chaos of accusing your best friend of being the traitor, or getting raided the moment you finish your DarkRP base.

🎢

Build a Rube Goldberg machine

Chain together buttons, dominoes, seesaws, and explosives so that pressing one switch sets off an absurd, over-engineered reaction.

👕

Design a PAC3 outfit

Spend an unreasonable amount of time building a custom look piece by piece — armor, a mech suit, floating props, whatever look you can imagine.

🧨

Stress-test your own builds

Built something sturdy? Throw explosives, cars, or an army of NPCs at it and see how much punishment it can take before it falls apart.
